In the wake of [my last essay on why I don't vibe code](/personal/why-i-dont-vibe-code), I heard from various
people on the Internet who read it (or the Bluesky skeets that inspired it).
Some had adopted similar stances on their own, often for overlapping reasons.
Others were dedicated vibe coders who wanted to share what practices they used
that made things manageable for them. I appreciate the feedback! I'm not going
to change my stance, but it's good to get perspective on how actual developers
(and not senior executives or marketing materials) have engaged with this
technology.

## Keep It Small And Focused

Based on multiple sources, it seems like the best approach for using LLMs to
write code is to keep them highly constrained in scope. Asking the agents to build out
entire features leads to an avalanche of code in sprawling PRs. 



To be honest, this seems like it's a fair amount of frustrating work.
